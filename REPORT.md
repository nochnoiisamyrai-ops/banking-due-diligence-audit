# 📑 Executive Summary: Commercial Bank M&A Due Diligence Audit

**Project Status:** Completed  
**Analysis Period:** 2023–2025  
**Target Scope:** Retail & corporate client portfolio, credit risk, operational metrics, and contingent liabilities.

---

## 🚦 Executive Dashboard ("Traffic Light" Status)

| Analytical Block | Status | Summary / Rationale |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Customer Base** | 🟡 **Requires Attention** | High churn spike at month 3; 24% of the database consists of "ghost" accounts. |
| **2. Balance & Deposits** | 🔴 **High Risk** | Critical concentration: Top 20 clients hold 42% of liabilities. |
| **3. Operations** | 🟢 **Normal** | Transaction architecture is stable; load peaks are predictable. |
| **4. Risks & Fraud** | 🟢 **Normal** | Fraud rate is within norms, though anti-fraud rules require tuning to lower false positives. |
| **5. Credit Portfolio** | 🔴 **High Risk** | Presence of hidden overdue loans and limit overruns in revolving credit lines. |
| **6. Loyalty Program** | 🟡 **Requires Attention** | Accumulated point liabilities are insufficiently provisioned. |

---

## 💡 Final Investment Recommendation

1. **Deal Verdict:** **Conditionally Recommended for Acquisition with a Discount.**
2. **Rationale:** The bank maintains a robust operational infrastructure and a high-value premium client base. However, identified liability concentrations and hidden asset quality issues in the credit portfolio necessitate a **7–10% Purchase Price Adjustment (PPA)**.
3. **Closing Conditions:**
   * Conduct due diligence on top 20 corporate depositors to secure rollover agreements upon ownership transition.
   * Establish additional provisions for potential losses across SME credit lines.
